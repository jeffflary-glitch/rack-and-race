What a data center technician does

The job in five moves

Two of these are already done at home. The rest is the same work at a bigger scale, done by the written steps.

  • RackMount servers on the rails of a 42U rack.
  • StackInstall drives, memory and cards in a machine.Done at home
  • CableRoute power and network cables, then label both ends.
  • SwapPull a failed part and fit the new one, like a GPU or RAM upgrade.Done at home
  • LogClose the ticket with exactly what was done.
